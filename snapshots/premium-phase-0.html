Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our security, turnaround times, and hand-crafted retouching workflow.

Do you use AI tools or automated filters for jewelry retouching?
Never. Automated AI tools distort prong geometry, blur hallmarks, and create unnatural gemstone facets. Every single image is retouched 100% manually by senior jewelry specialists using Wacom pen tablets, hand-drawn clipping paths, and custom dodge-and-burn techniques.
How is the confidentiality of our unreleased jewelry designs protected?
All client uploads, including free trial test images, are automatically protected under a strict Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Files are stored on encrypted, access-controlled servers and permanently purged 30 days after project delivery. We never showcase client work without explicit written consent.
What file formats do you accept and deliver?
We accept all camera RAW formats (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW), TIFF, PSD, PNG, and high-res JPEG, as well as raw 3D renders from MatrixGold, Rhino, and KeyShot. Final deliverables can include layered PSDs with separate metal/stone adjustment layers, transparent PNGs, and web-ready RGB (255,255,255) files.
How fast is your turnaround time for USA and European studios?
Our 24/7 production desk is aligned with EST, PST, GMT, and CET schedules. Standard turnaround is 24 hours for batches under 50 images, with a 12-hour express overnight option so you wake up to press-ready assets.
What if an image needs adjustments after delivery?
We offer unlimited complimentary revisions within 14 days of delivery. Whether you need warmer 18k rose gold tones, stronger natural contact shadows, or tighter cropping, our team refines it promptly at no extra cost.

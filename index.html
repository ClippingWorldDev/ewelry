Our jewelry retouching process has six steps: cut out, clean, add shine, align, match color, and check. Every photo goes through all six, in this order.

  • Space inside rings, chains, and links is cleared.
  • A soft contact shadow stays, so the piece does not float.
  • Delivered on white or transparent, as your store needs.

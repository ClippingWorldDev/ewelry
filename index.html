The Six Steps of Jewelry Retouching

From raw macro capture to museum-grade commercial imagery—six disciplined hand-finished stages calibrated for luxury maisons and fine jewelry houses.

01
01Vector IsolationStep 01 of 06

Background Removal

Every piece is traced at 400% magnification, preserving prongs, band openings and natural diamond edge detail.

  • Manual Bezier paths preserve delicate prongs and stone boundaries
  • Internal negative space isolated for rings, chains and articulated links
  • Natural contact shadow preserved to avoid artificial floating appearance
  • Delivered on pure white (RGB 255) or transparent layered alpha channel
Vector Path Cutout
02
02Basic RetouchingStep 02 of 06

Dust & Surface Flaw Removal

Fine dust, lint and micro-scratches are removed with controlled localized cleanup while retaining authentic metal grain.

  • Removal of airborne micro-dust, lint fibers and bench handling oils
  • Authentic metal grain retained without synthetic plastic smearing
  • Facet grease and surface scratches cleared to restore natural brilliance
  • Selective frequency separation isolating fine texture from underlying tone
Healing Surface Flaws
03
03Hand RetouchingStep 03 of 06

Sculpting 3D Luster

Manual dodge-and-burn reveals satin highlights, pavilion depth and natural specular light across metal and gemstones.

  • Hand-painted specular highlights along bezels, shanks and claws
  • Volumetric dodge-and-burn sculpting authentic 3D optical curvature
  • Calibrated facet scintillation boost restoring GIA-certified diamond fire
  • Balanced studio reflection gradients mirroring softbox light transitions
Sculpting 3D Luster

04Grid & AlignmentStep 04 of 06

Geometric Symmetry

Perspective, axis and center mass are audited before catalog use so every image aligns consistently across the collection.

  • Sub-millimeter horizontal and vertical axis alignment locking
  • Center of mass balanced for upright, stable catalog presentation
  • Prong and stone symmetry corrected from physical bench casting offsets
  • Standardized scale and margin proportions across all collection SKUs

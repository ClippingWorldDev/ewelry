Diamond Retouching

Why 400% Magnification & Manual Wacom Paths Outperform AI on Fine Jewelry

Generative filters hallucinate prong shapes and soften engraved hallmarks. Discover how our bench specialists preserve carat geometry while eliminating studio dust and tripod reflections.

BJR Senior Bench Nov 22, 2026 6 min read 400% QC Protocol

Every luxury jewelry house faces the same dilemma once macro studio captures arrive on the workstation: raw lenses reveal micro-dust, wax residue, harsh softbox reflections, and uneven metallic transitions — yet automated AI retouching alters the piece itself.

At 400% magnification, the difference between an algorithmic filter and a dedicated human jewelry specialist becomes unmistakable. Automated tools blur tiny 750 gold hallmarks, distort prong symmetry, and invent artificial diamond facets that misrepresent the true stone cut.

01 Hand-Drawn Vector Isolation vs. Automated Cutouts

Delicate chains, pavé prongs, and openwork galleries demand surgical Pen Tool precision. Rather than relying on feather-edged AI masks that leave gray halos on white backgrounds, our specialists trace every curve manually onto pure RGB 255 white or custom editorial surfaces while preserving grounded contact shadows.

“In fine jewelry, authenticity is non-negotiable. A collector inspects every prong and facet before trusting a maison online.”
— BJR Haute Atelier Bench Standards

02 Calibrated Dodge-and-Burn Metallic Sculpting

Precious metals act like curved mirrors in the studio. Instead of flat frequency-separation smoothing, we sculpt directional specular gradients across 18K Yellow Gold, Rose Gold, and Platinum so reflections look naturally liquid and uninterrupted.

Atelier Bench Quality Checklist

  • 400% zoom inspection across all prongs & pavé
  • 16-bit layered PSD with non-destructive masks
  • Calibrated sRGB, Adobe RGB & Display P3
  • 256-bit encrypted vault & Mutual NDA

03 Deliverables Ready for Print & Global E-Commerce

Every finished master is exported alongside web-optimized crops and color-checked across calibrated monitors before overnight delivery synchronized to New York, London, Paris, and Geneva time zones.

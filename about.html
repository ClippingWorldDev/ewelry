Our Philosophy

Why We Never Trust Jewelry to Automated AI

Automated retouching tools flatten metallic transitions, warp prong symmetry, blur artisan hallmarks, and invent artificial stone facets that misrepresent the true piece.

For a collector or bride evaluating a high-value ring or timepiece online, authenticity is everything. Our specialists preserve every genuine hallmark and stone cut while removing only studio dust, micro-scratches, harsh tripod reflections, and wax residues.

Every deliverable arrives in calibrated 16-bit layered PSD and web-ready formats matched strictly to your house color profile (sRGB, Adobe RGB, or Display P3).

Bench Specialists

Four Disciplines Inside Every Frame

Rather than passing files through a single generalist, your imagery moves across four dedicated finishing benches.

01

400% Vector & Pathing Masters

Hand-drawn Pen Tool clipping paths isolate delicate chains, filigree galleries, and pavé edges onto pure RGB 255 white or custom editorial backdrops while preserving natural contact shadows.

02

Gemstone & Facet Fire Artists

Micro-contrast calibration restores diamond scintillation, emerald depth, and sapphire color fidelity without synthetic over-saturation or unnatural sparkle overlays.

03

Metallic Luster Sculptors

Custom dodge-and-burn gradient sculpting eliminates camera reflections and enables true-to-assay digital recasting across 18K Yellow Gold, Rose Gold, Platinum, and Black Rhodium.

04

CAD & Horology Finishers

Raw MatrixGold and KeyShot 3D renders, alongside Swiss timepiece dials and bezels, are refined with authentic micro-textures and studio-grade specular balance.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 — Your jewelry assets are sacred. This policy outlines how we protect your intellectual property, unreleased collections, and personal data with military-grade encryption and zero AI exposure.

01

Information We Collect

When you submit a free trial request, private inquiry, or production batch, we collect only the contact details (name, brand/studio name, email address) and project files necessary to complete and deliver your retouching work.

We do not collect browsing analytics, install tracking cookies, or share any data with advertising networks.

02

Zero AI Training & Asset Confidentiality

Your raw RAW/TIFF/PSD jewelry files are never uploaded to public generative AI models, third-party training datasets, or external stock libraries.

Every file is processed manually by our in-house bench specialists under strict mutual NDA protection inside encrypted, air-gapped studio workstations.

03

File Storage & Automatic Purge

Project assets are transferred via 256-bit encrypted channels and stored only for the duration of active production and your agreed archive window (default: 30 days post-delivery).

Clients may request immediate permanent deletion of all raw and master files at any time via email.

04

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade client contact information or proprietary jewelry photography with any third party. Your brand identity and collection imagery remain exclusively yours.

05

Your Privacy Rights & Contact

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing studio@bestjewelryretouching.com. We respond to all data requests within 48 business hours.

Your assets are protected by our automatic mutual NDA.

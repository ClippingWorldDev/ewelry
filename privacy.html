Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • BestJewelryRetouching.com

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a free trial request, private inquiry, or production batch, we collect only the contact details (name, brand/studio name, email address) and project files necessary to complete and deliver your retouching work.

2. Zero AI Training & Asset Confidentiality

Your raw RAW/TIFF/PSD jewelry files are never uploaded to public generative AI models, third-party training datasets, or external stock libraries. Every file is processed manually by our in-house bench specialists under strict mutual NDA protection.

3. File Storage & Automatic Purge

Project assets are transferred via 256-bit encrypted channels and stored only for the duration of active production and your agreed archive window. Clients may request immediate permanent deletion of all raw and master files at any time.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade client contact information or proprietary jewelry photography with any third party.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Contact

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing studio@bestjewelryretouching.com.

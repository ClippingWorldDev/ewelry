1. Scope of Retouching Services

BestJewelryRetouching.com (“BJR Haute Atelier”) provides manual, human-crafted jewelry post-production services including vector background isolation, surface dust and flaw cleanup, dodge-and-burn luster sculpting, geometric alignment, and digital metal alloy recoloring. All deliverables are produced to the agreed project brief and tier specifications.

2. Intellectual Property & Client Ownership

Clients retain 100% exclusive copyright and ownership over all raw captures, layered 16-bit PSD master files, and final exported assets. BJR Haute Atelier never claims ownership over client jewelry designs, unreleased collections, or brand assets, and will never publish or showcase client work without explicit written permission.

3. Mutual Non-Disclosure & Vault Security

Every engagement is automatically governed by our mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Unreleased bridal, high-jewelry, and timepiece assets are handled exclusively inside encrypted, access-controlled studio workstations with zero third-party AI training or public cloud exposure.

4. Turnaround Times & Revisions

Standard turnaround SLA is 24–48 hours depending on batch volume and complexity tier. Complimentary revisions are included to ensure exact alignment with your brand’s color space (sRGB, Adobe RGB, or Display P3) and artistic direction.

5. Billing, Free Trial & Cancellations

New luxury houses and studios are eligible for two (2) complimentary test retouches prior to commercial engagement. Commercial invoices are issued upon project approval or under agreed monthly retainer schedules.

6. Contact Desk

For legal or SLA inquiries, please contact studio@bestjewelryretouching.com.

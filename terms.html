Atelier Legal Protocol

Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 — These terms govern every engagement with BestJewelryRetouching.com, including our mutual NDA, intellectual property rights, and service-level commitments.

01

Scope of Retouching Services

BestJewelryRetouching.com (“BJR Haute Atelier”) provides manual, human-crafted jewelry post-production services including vector background isolation, surface dust and flaw cleanup, dodge-and-burn luster sculpting, geometric alignment, digital metal alloy recoloring, and 3D CAD render enhancement.

All deliverables are produced to the agreed project brief and tier specifications (Essentials, Editorial & Macro, or Haute Couture).

02

Intellectual Property & Client Ownership

Clients retain 100% exclusive copyright and ownership over all raw captures, layered 16-bit PSD master files, and final exported assets. BJR Haute Atelier never claims ownership over client jewelry designs, unreleased collections, or brand assets.

We will never publish, feature, or showcase client work without explicit written permission.

03

Mutual Non-Disclosure & Vault Security

Every engagement is automatically governed by our mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Unreleased bridal, high-jewelry, and timepiece assets are handled exclusively inside 256-bit encrypted, access-controlled studio workstations.

Zero third-party AI training, public cloud exposure, or unauthorized sharing of your proprietary assets.

04

Turnaround Times & Revisions

Standard turnaround SLA is 24–48 hours depending on batch volume and complexity tier. Rush delivery options are available for time-sensitive lookbook launches and campaign deadlines.

Complimentary revisions are included to ensure exact alignment with your brand’s color space (sRGB, Adobe RGB, or Display P3) and artistic direction.

05

Billing, Free Trial & Cancellations

New luxury houses and studios are eligible for two (2) complimentary test retouches prior to commercial engagement. No credit card or upfront payment is required for the trial.

Commercial invoices are issued upon project approval or under agreed monthly retainer schedules. Projects may be canceled before production begins at no cost.

06

Contact Desk

For legal, SLA, or service inquiries, please contact studio@bestjewelryretouching.com.
